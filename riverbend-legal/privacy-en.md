# Riverbend — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 30 July 2026**

Riverbend is a single-player fishing game made by Esquyna Labs. This policy
describes what the app does with information. It is short because the app does
very little.

## The short version

**Riverbend collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing about you
anywhere except on your own device.**

## Data we collect

None.

Riverbend has no user accounts, no sign-in, no registration and no contact
form. It never asks for your name, e-mail address, telephone number, date of
birth or location. It does not read your contacts, your photos, your calendar,
your microphone or your camera.

## Data we share

None, because there is none to share. Riverbend contains no advertising
network, no analytics package, no crash-reporting service, no attribution SDK
and no third-party software development kit of any kind that transmits data.

## Network access

Riverbend makes no network connections.

On Android this is enforced by the operating system rather than promised by us:
the app does not declare the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission, so the
Android runtime will refuse any attempt to open a network connection from the
app, whether or not the app tried to make one.

## Data stored on your device

Riverbend writes two things into its own private storage area:

* **your save** — fishing level and experience, the species you have caught and
  their record sizes, your coins, inventory and equipped rod, bait and tackle,
  and the in-game date, time and weather;
* **your settings** — sound and haptics on or off, and similar preferences.

Both live in the app's private directory. Other apps cannot read them. They are
never uploaded. Deleting Riverbend deletes them.

## Device backup

On Android, the operating system's own backup service may include an app's
private files in a backup of your device. If it does, those files are handled
by Google under Google's policies, not ours, and Riverbend is not involved and
receives nothing. The same is true of an iCloud or iTunes backup on iOS. This
is your device's behaviour, not a transmission by Riverbend, and you can turn
it off in your device's own backup settings.

## Permissions the app requests

* **Vibration** (Android: `android.permission.VIBRATE`) — used to make the
  controller buzz when a fish bites and when the line strains. It grants no
  access to any information. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings,
  and denying vibration affects nothing else.

Riverbend requests no other permission on either platform.

## Advertising identifiers

Riverbend does not use the Android Advertising ID, Apple's `IDFA`, Apple's
`identifierForVendor`, the Android ID, the device serial number, the IMEI, or
any other device or advertising identifier.

## Children

Riverbend collects no personal information from anybody, and therefore collects
none from children. It contains no advertising, no purchases, no chat, no
user-generated content, no social features and no links out of the app.

## Purchases

Riverbend contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. If you paid for
the app, that transaction was between you and Google or Apple; we never see
your payment details.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. A
change that meant Riverbend started collecting something would be announced in
the app's release notes as well.
